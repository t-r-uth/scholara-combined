## Design Context

### Users
Senior academics and professors (primarily Malaysian universities, e.g. Universiti Malaya). They use Scholara during working hours to find research collaborators. Context: focused, professional, credibility-conscious. They are evaluating whether this platform is trustworthy enough to put their ORCID identity on.

### Brand Personality
Clean, trustworthy, modern.

Physical analogy: a well-designed European research council's digital presence — precise, generous whitespace, every typographic choice considered. Nothing decorative without purpose.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light
- **Tone**: Refined authority. Not startup-casual. Not academic-stuffy. The intersection of precision and approachability.
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS dashboards (Tailwind templates, shadcn defaults), overly playful edtech, glassmorphism.
- **Typography**: Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Hanken Grotesk (body). Both chosen deliberately — not in the AI reflex list. Grotesque pairing gives a modern, considered feel without the tech-bro associations of Inter/DM Sans.
- **Color**: OKLCH palette. Neutrals tinted toward the blue accent (hue ~250). Trust-anchored blue as the single accent. ORCID green (#A6CE39) as a fixed brand color — never used as UI accent.

### Design Principles
1. **Credentials first** — ORCID identity is the trust signal; treat it with authority, not as a footnote.
2. **Whitespace is structure** — let sections breathe; density signals low quality in academic contexts.
3. **Typography carries hierarchy** — rely on type scale and weight contrast, not borders and cards, for visual organization.
4. **One accent, used rarely** — blue only for interactive elements and the ORCID link. Never decorative.
5. **Forms feel like tools** — inputs are precise instruments, not afterthoughts. Focus states are clear and confident.
